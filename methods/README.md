# Methods

Method files compose a baseline with multiple practices into a complete methodology. Each method references its baseline via `baselinePracticeName` and lists constituent practices by name in `practiceNames`. When rendered in Keleo Studio, all Alphas, Activities, WorkProducts, and Patterns are merged into a single composite view.

---

## Team Topologies

Organizational design methodology enabling fast flow through team types, interaction modes, cognitive load management, and continuous organizational sensing. Composes all three Team Topologies practices on top of the Platform Adoption Essentials baseline.

The composite view adds practice-specific Alphas (highlighted in blue below) to the baseline structure: **Team Topology Design** and **Mental Bandwidth** (Cognitive Load) under Endeavor/Team, **Interaction Pattern** (Team Interaction Mode) under Endeavor/Way Of Working, and **Sensing** (Organizational Sensing) and **Team Topology Design** under Endeavor/Organizational Change.

![Team Topologies -- Overview of Concerns](team%20topologies.png)

**Baseline:** Platform Adoption Essentials
**Practices:** Team Structure & Cognitive Load, Organizational Evolution & Sensing, Team Topologies Lifecycle
**File:** [`team-topologies.json`](team-topologies.json)

## Platform Engineering

A comprehensive platform engineering methodology built on the IDP Essentials baseline (which itself extends Platform Adoption Essentials with developer-experience-specific terminology). Combines the CNCF Platform Engineering practice with the Team Topologies suite, producing a rich composite that covers platform product management, developer portals, capability domains, team topology design, cognitive load, organizational sensing, and platform measurement.

The composite view shows IDP-specific Alphas from the baseline (Internal Developer Platform, Developer Portal, Developer Experience Needs, Workload) alongside practice contributions (highlighted in blue): **Platform Product Management** and **Platform Investment Model** under Value, **Capability Domain** under Solution, and **Mental Bandwidth**, **Interaction Pattern**, **Platform Measurement Practice**, **Platform Adoption Journey**, **Sensing**, and **Team Topology Design** under Endeavor.

![Platform Engineering -- Overview of Concerns](platform%20engineering.png)

**Baseline:** IDP Essentials
**Practices:** Platform Engineering (CNCF), Team Topologies Lifecycle, Organizational Evolution & Sensing, Team Structure & Cognitive Load
**File:** [`platform-engineering.json`](platform-engineering.json)
